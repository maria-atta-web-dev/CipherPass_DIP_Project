SLIDE 2 — WHAT IS KYC?
-----------------------
Title: What is KYC?

KYC = Know Your Customer
- Bank verifies identity BEFORE opening account
- Pakistan: NADRA CNIC (front + back), face, signature
- AML = Anti-Money Laundering: check criminal/fraud lists

Our system automates: CNIC scan -> enhance (DIP) -> criminal check -> face -> decision

SLIDE 3 — INDUSTRY USE (PAKISTAN)
----------------------------------
Title: Where Is This Used in Pakistan?

- Banks: HBL, UBL, Meezan Bank — account opening at branch
- Mobile wallets: JazzCash, Easypaisa — CNIC + selfie verification
- NADRA: National ID (CNIC) is the core identity document
- SBP: State Bank requires KYC & Anti-Money Laundering (AML) checks
- FIA: Federal Investigation Agency — Criminal & financial crime screening

One line: Before opening an account, the bank must know WHO the customer is
and IF they are a fraud/criminal risk.

SLIDE 4 — PROBLEM STATEMENT
-----------------------------
Title: Problem Statement

Manual KYC in Pakistani banks:
- Slow queues at branches
- Fake CNIC and forged documents
- No instant link to FIA/criminal history
- Cannot detect duplicate/repeat fraud attempts
- SBP penalties if AML checks are skipped

Our solution: Automated CNIC + face + document check with criminal record lookup,
duplicate detection, and DIP-based forgery analysis.

SLIDE 7 — DIP CONCEPTS (MAIN ACADEMIC FOCUS)
----------------------------------------------
Title: Digital Image Processing Concepts Used

#  | Concept                 | Use in Project
---|-------------------------|------------------------------------------
1  | Grayscale conversion    | All modules — standardize image format
2  | Histogram Equalization  | Face detection — fix poor lighting
3  | Gaussian Blur           | Noise reduction before edge detection
4  | Canny Edge Detection    | CNIC boundary detection & forgery check
5  | CLAHE                   | Enhance local contrast on CNIC images
6  | Laplacian Variance      | Liveness check — detect blurry/printed photos
7  | Haar Cascade            | Real-time face bounding boxes on webcam
8  | Contour Detection       | Document shape and region analysis
9  | Perspective Transform   | CNIC deskewing and alignment

SLIDE 8 — CRIMINAL RECORD & AML (FIA/PAKISTAN)
------------------------------------------------
Title: AML Screening — Criminal & Fraud Database

When CNIC is entered:
- Lookup bank customer database (citizen_database.json)
- Lookup criminal/fraud watchlist (criminal_records.json — FIA-style demo data)
- Count previous verifications — 3+ attempts = DUPLICATE FRAUD ALERT
- Decision: APPROVE | MANUAL REVIEW | REJECT

Compliance Officer Actions:
- REJECT       — Block account opening
- ESCALATE     — Send to branch manager
- INVESTIGATE  — Request more documents
- REPORT_FIA   — Report to Federal Investigation Agency
- HOLD         — Pending further review
